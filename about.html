WHO WE ARE

Making Fridge Repairs Simple and Straightforward

At CoolFix Fridge Repairs, we understand how important a working refrigerator or freezer is to everyday life. When an appliance stops cooling properly, makes unusual noises or develops a fault, it can quickly become a major inconvenience.

That's why our focus is on providing helpful service and practical repair solutions for both households and businesses. We aim to make the repair process easier by listening to your concerns, explaining the next steps and helping you understand your options.

Whether you need help with a household fridge, a freezer or commercial refrigeration equipment, CoolFix is here to help you make an informed repair enquiry.
